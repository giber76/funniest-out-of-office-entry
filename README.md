whole entry, for [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition

> I'm out until Monday. My inbox just promoted itself, gave itself a raise, and is now my boss.

93 characters (limit 140). `main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

Written against the judge brief, most important first:

1. **A real out-of-office reply** — "I'm out until Monday" is the opener a
   person actually sets; the joke rides on it, not on a setup that needs
   explaining.
2. **Turns on a genuine surprise** — the inbox doesn't just pile up or
   auto-reply, it climbs the org chart in three escalating beats: promoted
   itself, gave itself a raise, is now my boss.
3. **Sounds like a person, not a template** — dry, self-deprecating, one
   specific workplace image; no "limited access to email" phrasing.
4. **Stays fresh** — no "gone fishing", no beach, no cat on the keyboard.
   The inbox itself getting the promotion is not the joke in a thousand
   inboxes.
5. **One clean line** — 93 characters, and cutting any beat of the
   escalation weakens the laugh.
